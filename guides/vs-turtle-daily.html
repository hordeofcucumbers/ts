VS = Alliance Duel TT = Turbo Turtle PP = Power Play (4h, CRL times) Green = counts in VS + Turtle → spend it NEW = added 23 Nov check = VS list not yet seen in game

The golden rules

  • Spend an item only when it is green: it counts in VS and Turtle that day.
  • Best: spend it in the Power Play window for the same item (triple points).
  • Triple times are CRL-specific. Power Play timing depends on the alliance. Other alliances: check your own Power Play calendar.
  • Diamond bundles count every VS day and every Turtle stage.

Sunday No VS · TT Stage 7 (everything counts)

Turtle Stage 7

  • Signature gear fragments, all gear, reforge

  • Drafts, recruits, fragments, manuals

  • General parts, precision blueprints

  • Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun: do but don't claim. Claim only the triangle and wandering-zombie missions.
  • Aim for about 30 held missions before Monday.
