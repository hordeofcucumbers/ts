Quick start

The six things to do first, and the twelve rules to keep.

Always have a builder on the Power Plant or a building it needs. Most buildings cannot go above the Power Plant level.
Choose five heroes and use your resources on them only. Start with Stalwarts: Rosie, then Tara, Nikola, Tarzan, Ray.
Upgrade each of the five heroes to 8★ before you upgrade any hero further. 8★ unlocks the hero's talent.
Give the main squad gold gear. Then add stars to all pieces at the same pace: all to 2★, then 4★, then 6★.
Use speedups on the VS day that gives points for them. Construction and pets on Tuesday, research on Wednesday, hero items on Thursday, signature gear and Chief Collection on Friday.
Send every squad except the main squad to gather. Do not gather on Saturday.

12 rules

1

Focus on one squad at a time

Put gear, skills and signature weapons into one squad of five heroes before you start the next squad.

2

Five heroes of one faction (5/0)

After T10, faction research gives a bonus to one faction only. About 350K medals per branch.

3

Four heroes to 8★ before any 10★

8★ costs 1,300 fragments. 10★ costs 1,990.

4

Gold gear first, then stars

The first star (0★→1★) gives the most power for one gold piece.

5

Level purple gear, use it on gold

The gold piece gets all the levels. Levelled gold into gold loses about half.

6

Power Plant first

Keep Lab 1, Comms Outpost and Barracks 1 one level below it.

7

Turn on the buff, then start

Buffs only work on jobs you start after you turn them on.

8

Spend on double-point days

When VS and Turbo Turtle both give points, you score in both. See the VS + Turtle daily plan.

9

Main squad stays home

Send the other squads to gather.

10

No gathering on Saturday

Enemies attack gathering squads and get points from them.

11

Empty the garrison before fights

Settlement → Barricade → remove all teams except the main squad. If you are offline, remove the main squad as well to lower the chance of losses.

12

Collect the free bonuses

Ferris Wheel fragments, permanent skins and Lab speedup boxes.

Common mistakes

  • Upgrading every hero you get.
  • One gear piece at 6★ while the others stay at 0★.
  • Levelling a gold piece and then using it on another gold piece.
  • Weapons at L3 on several heroes. Get one to L5 first.
  • Speedups on buildings the Power Plant does not need.
  • Starting a build or research before the buff is on.
  • Using speedups on a day VS gives no points for them.
  • Gathering on Saturday.
  • Leaving teams in the garrison during fights.
  • Researching one faction, then changing faction.

Upgrade order

Gold gear on Rosie (or your main damage dealer), then the rest of the main squad. Purple gear on everyone else.
Level with bolts to multiples of 10 (L10, L20 …). These levels give the largest power increase.
Stars at the same pace: all pieces to 2★, then 4★, then 6★. Each opens a special-stat line.
Reforge to orange, keep MAX lines. Match the stats to the hero's role.
Keep one purple set in reserve for the next new hero.
Level purple gear and use it to upgrade gold gear. The gold piece gets the full benefit of the levels. Do not upgrade gold gear with levelled gold gear. You get back only about half the levels, and the bolts come back as white bolts, which give no event points.

How weapons work

Skill in three parts

A base effect that grows with level, an extra effect at L10 and another at L15.

Who gets the bonus

"All Heroes" in the text = every hero in the squad. No "all" = that hero only. A weapon never helps other squads.

Stats

At L5 each weapon adds 9.36K attack, 1.87K defence and 624K health to its hero (257K power).

Best practices

Buy weapons only for heroes who stay in your main squad.
Upgrade one weapon to L5 before you start the next. One at L5 and one at L1 is more useful than two at L3.
"All heroes" weapons first, then the weapon of your main damage dealer.
Then L10 and L15 for the extra effects. In the end every main-squad hero has a weapon.
Changing faction? The Hero Development Exchange event moves weapon levels between two SSR heroes. At least one weapon must be L5 or higher.

Upgrade order

Stalwarts

  1. Rosie: attack + defence, all heroes
  2. Tara: defence, all heroes
  3. Tarzan: defence, all heroes
  4. Nikola: own defence
  5. Ray: own attack

With Lava: Rosie's guitar and Lava's claws first.

  2. Chiron: attack + defence, all heroes

  4. Layla: own attack and defence

Mariners

  1. Undine: the only Mariner weapon for all heroes. L15 first
  2. Others: L10 each

When to buy

New heroes arrive about every 2 weeks: week 1 the hero, week 2 its weapon. The launch offer does not come back in the same form, so save weapon coins before the weapon week.

Weapon coins

Turbo Turtle daily chest at 10M points (weapon fragments), Turtle rank rewards, the Reservoir shop and event shops. If you spend money, players put titans before weapons.

Signature gear fragments

Since 23 Nov they give points. Use them on Friday: 266,600 VS points each, and Turtle counts them too. Monday also counts in both.

Builders and buffs

Free second builder

Rent it for 24 h in Turbo Turtle. If the rental ends during a build, the build still finishes. Start your longest build just before it ends.

Buff first

Governor's Chief Strategist (+10%) and the season +15% skill only work on jobs started after them.

Free speedups

Each Infected Monster help box: 5 min, chance of 15 min. Arkadia and State Clash stages: 20 h.

Research order and strategy: see chapter 8.

Economy and defence

Gathering

All squads except main. 24 h gathering bonus (up-arrow → Development → Gathering Bonus, 600 diamonds). The alliance Harvest skill adds to it.

Survivors

Upgrade interiors of houses, kitchen and production buildings. Soup only. Complete requests. Over 70% mood they send gifts. Keep needs above 30%.

Free boosts

Ferris Wheel: 9 fragments from re-exploring island locations, L1 = +20% defence. Bonuses of all permanent skins apply at the same time.

Defence

Before Arkadia, Reservoir or Saturday VS: remove all teams except main from the garrison. Keep at least one squad at home.

Late game and seasons

Golden statues

Warbringer (PP14), Golden Aegis (PP17), Guardian of Life (PP18). Each to L30. Statue level needs the same Power Plant level.

Steles (PP30)

Blessed Healing: more of your lost troops go to hospital. Cursed Wound: more enemy troops die.

Season buildings

Main building and 4 production buildings to L30 first, others at L1. Main building L26 gives SR→SSR coins.

Research roadmap: what to research next

Follow the phases from top to bottom. Each phase starts when its condition is met. Keep the Lab busy at all times.

1

First week: speed

From the start
  • Development: research speed and construction speed nodes.
  • Heroes: research the branch to 57%.
Next when Heroes reaches 57% → Infected Conquest unlocks
▼
2

Weeks 1 to 2: free speedups

Heroes 57%
  • Infected Conquest: "Unholy infected: speedup items" to +1 or +2 (green 5-minute boxes), then "Infected: speedup items" (white 1-minute boxes) and Random Box.
  • Economic: gathering skills in between. Skip production.
Next when Lab 1 reaches L10 → Alliance Duel unlocks
▼
3

Alliance Duel: better rewards

Lab 1 L10
  • Research the path to the last skill (7 to 9 reward chests) as fast as you can.
  • Take Competition Master to the maximum on the way.
  • Intel Post in gaps: Signal Boost (both) and Storage Increase. You can store up to 41 Intel Missions, so save them for VS days.
Next when Duel reaches 66% and Lab 1 reaches L15 → Special Forces unlocks
▼
4

Special Forces: the road to T10

Duel 66% + Lab 1 L15
  • Research the whole branch (248K medals). Start new levels on Wednesdays, with speedups from Random Boxes.
  • On other days: Duel first row (radar, speedups, hero summons) to 10/10, and the rest of Economic gathering.
  • Choose your long-term faction now, before T10.
Next when Special Forces reaches 100% → T10 and faction research unlock
▼
5

Faction power: one faction

Special Forces 100%
  • Research the branch of your main squad's faction only (376K medals, 2 to 3 seasons).
  • Past 50%, a 5/0 squad of that faction is clearly the strongest.

Later, when Barracks 1 is about L22 to L26

Development: hospital and barracks nodes, Alliance Research and Alliance Construction, Efficient Healing and Efficient Training (from T9), Survival Training. Then Troops and the Intel Post filter skills (fewer blue, more gold missions).

Skip

Doomsday Express while medals can go into T10 or your faction. Troops: Power Training. Economic: production skills (except Resource Production in seasons). A second faction branch.

Research strategy

  • Turn on buffs first. Research buffs only work on research you start after the buff.
  • Spend Duel coins and medals on Wednesday. Points count on the day you spend them, not the day the research finishes.
  • Random Box is the main free source of speedups for Special Forces. Clear 100 infected every day.
  • Choose your faction before T10. One faction branch takes 2 to 3 seasons of active play. Medals for two branches are not realistic.
  • Faction research does not apply to Mariners in another faction's squad.
  • A second Lab slot comes only with paid offers.
